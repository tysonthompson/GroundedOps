Document: Remote Work Guidelines
Owner: People Operations

Employees may work remotely from approved locations where the company is registered to employ staff. Employees must update their work location in the HR system before working from a new state, province, or country.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's primary team time zone, Monday through Thursday. Teams may define additional coverage expectations for customer-facing launches or operational incidents.

Employees working remotely must use company-managed devices for company work. Personal devices may not be used to store customer data, employee records, source code, or confidential planning documents.

Managers should evaluate remote employees based on outcomes, communication quality, and reliability rather than hours visibly online.

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 business days per calendar year with approval from People Operations, Legal, and their manager.
