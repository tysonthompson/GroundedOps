Document: Security Access Policy
Owner: Security Engineering

Access to production systems is granted only to employees with a documented business need. Access requests must be submitted through the identity management system and approved by both the employee's manager and the system owner.

Production access reviews occur every 90 days. System owners must remove access for employees who no longer require it.

Employees must use multi-factor authentication for all systems that contain customer data, financial records, employee information, source code, or operational credentials.

Shared accounts are prohibited unless Security Engineering grants a temporary exception for a specific operational incident. Temporary shared-account exceptions expire after seven days.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to Security Operations within one hour of discovery. Security Operations may remotely lock or wipe devices that could expose company or customer data.

Contractors receive the minimum access necessary to complete their assignment. Contractor access expires automatically on the contract end date unless renewed by the sponsor.
