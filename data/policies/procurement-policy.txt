Document: Procurement Policy
Owner: Business Operations

Purchases under 1,000 USD may be approved by a direct manager if they are included in the team's budget.

Purchases from 1,000 USD to 10,000 USD require approval from the department head and Finance Operations.

Purchases above 10,000 USD require approval from the department head, Finance Operations, and Legal. Competitive vendor review is required for purchases above 25,000 USD unless a sole-source exception is approved.

New software tools that store customer data, employee data, source code, credentials, or financial records must complete a security review before purchase.

Annual renewals should be reviewed at least 45 days before the renewal date. The business owner must confirm continued need, active usage, and budget availability.
