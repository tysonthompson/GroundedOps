Document: Incident Response Playbook
Owner: Reliability Engineering

An incident is any unplanned event that materially degrades customer experience, compromises security, or threatens business operations.

Severity 1 incidents include total production outage, confirmed customer data exposure, or payment processing failure affecting more than 25 percent of active customers. Severity 1 incidents require an incident commander, communications lead, and executive sponsor.

The incident commander owns coordination, timeline updates, and role assignment. The communications lead owns internal updates every 30 minutes for Severity 1 incidents and every 60 minutes for Severity 2 incidents.

Post-incident reviews must be completed within five business days for Severity 1 incidents and within ten business days for Severity 2 incidents. The review should identify contributing factors, customer impact, detection gaps, and follow-up owners.

Incident notes should be factual and blameless. The purpose of review is learning and prevention, not punishment.
